## Thesis
Beyond Earth, radiation never stops; MARSOPS engineers the protection that lets people go farther and stay longer.
Told in the site's own language, so the teaser and the site read as one brand.

Allowed patterns: the site's palette, type and layout grammar (left-aligned Manrope headlines at the top third, a
muted line with a blue rule, tilted navy cards, thin lines, outline words); the site's own Mars; real NASA imagery in
natural colour; radiation as warm-white streaks (the environment's colour), protection in electric blue only;
text lines rising out of masks; one dissolve into the logo.
Not allowed: sepia/cyanotype grading, orchestral music, script fonts, centred headlines (the logo only), glow on
anything but the shield, camera pans/zooms on transitions.

## Motion identity
Precise premium. Signature curve cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). Durations: text line 0.7 s (stagger 0.08 s), exits
0.4 s, objects 1.2-1.4 s, logo 0.8 s. Entrance pattern: every text line rises out of its own mask. The camera
breathes (slow push); objects move with cause (Mars pushes in when the radiation arrives, sinks when the card rises).
