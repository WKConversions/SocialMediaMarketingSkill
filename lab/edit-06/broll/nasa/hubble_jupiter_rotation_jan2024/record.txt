Catalogue text (quoted): NASA Science record: "The Hubble Space Telescope images used in this animated science visualization present a full rotation of the giant planet Jupiter. This is not a real-time movie. Instead, Hubble snapshots of the colorful planet, taken January 5-6, 2024, have been photo-mapped onto a sphere, and the model is then rotated in animation." Credit: NASA, ESA, Amy Simon (NASA-GSFC).

What is visible: Colour Jupiter globe centred on black (1920x1080) turning through one full rotation; Great Red Spot comes and goes. Small white title text at top for about the first 4 s ("Hubble Space Telescope - Observations of Jupiter January 5-6, 2024"). Real Hubble snapshots photo-mapped onto a sphere and rotated: a visualisation of real data, not real-time footage.
