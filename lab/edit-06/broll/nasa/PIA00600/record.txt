Family Portrait of Jupiter's Great Red Spot and the Galilean Satellites (PIA00600)
Date: 1996 (Galileo images; Callisto from Voyager 1979); released 1997-11-18
Source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA00600 ; https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA00600 (file: https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/PIA00600/PIA00600~orig.jpg)
Licence: Public domain in the US (NASA/JPL media are generally not copyrighted; NASA Media Usage Guidelines: credit NASA, do not imply endorsement). Credit line: NASA/JPL/DLR

Catalogue text (quoted): NASA record: "This 'family portrait,' a composite of the Jovian system, includes the edge of Jupiter with its Great Red Spot, and Jupiter's four largest moons, known as the Galilean satellites. From top to bottom, the moons shown are Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto ... the portrait of Callisto is from the 1979 flyby of NASA's Voyager spacecraft." Secondary creator: NASA/JPL/DLR.

What is visible: Portrait-format composite (1053x1491) with a thin white frame line: left edge of Jupiter with the Great Red Spot, and the four Galilean moons stacked on the right, top to bottom Io (yellow-orange), Europa (pale, streaked), Ganymede (grey, large), Callisto (dark, bright craters). A montage, not a single photo: moons and planet are pasted together at a common scale.
