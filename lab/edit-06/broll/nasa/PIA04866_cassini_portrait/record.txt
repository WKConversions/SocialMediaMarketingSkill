Cassini Jupiter Portrait (PIA04866)
Date: 2000-12-29 (released 2003-11-13)
Source: https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA04866 (file: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/photojournal/pia/pia04/pia04866/PIA04866.tif)

Catalogue text (quoted): Photojournal record: "This true color mosaic of Jupiter was constructed from images taken by the narrow angle camera onboard NASA's Cassini spacecraft on December 29, 2000, during its closest approach ... It is the most detailed global color portrait of Jupiter ever produced". Credits: NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute.

What is visible: Real Cassini narrow-angle true-colour mosaic of Jupiter, portrait 1920x2400: the lit left part of the globe against black space, banded clouds, Great Red Spot lower centre, white ovals; right side falls into night. No moons in frame. Best portrait-friendly hero shot.
