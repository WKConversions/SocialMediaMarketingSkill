Io in Front of Jupiter (PIA02860)

Catalogue text (quoted): Photojournal record: "Jupiter's four largest satellites, including Io, the golden ornament in front of Jupiter in this image from NASA's Cassini spacecraft, have fascinated Earthlings ever since Galileo Galilei discovered them in 1610 in one of his first astronomical uses of the telescope ... This true-color composite frame, made from narrow angle images taken on Dec. 12, 2000, captures Io and its shadow in transit against the disk of Jupiter." Credits: NASA/JPL/University of Arizona.

What is visible: Real Cassini true-colour frame: lower half of Jupiter fills the top three-quarters (Great Red Spot left), with the small orange-white moon Io right of centre and Io's black shadow just up-left of it on the clouds; black space along the bottom. A still of one moon in transit, not the full set of four.
