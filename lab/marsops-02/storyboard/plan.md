# MARSOPS launch teaser v2: plan

## Brief (Karl, 2026-11-19, after v1)
"More in the old style" of the story videos (Wright 1903, Eiffel, Christmas 1882/1843): real historical material, the
same feeling, though with no voice-over, script or presenter. Keep the blueprint drawing, combined with it. Built with
what the skill does rather than freestyled: real archival sources with rights checks, found music (free, downloadable,
no copyright) cut to the film, real recorded sound effects (no synthesized ones). Same base brief: 15 s, 9:16
(1080x1920), Leonardo / renaissance sketches coming to life, the mission of protecting astronauts against radiation,
dark blue, white type, subtle neon-blue accents, serious and modern, ending on "MARSOPS - Coming soon".

## Thesis
Every leap into the unknown began as a drawing: from a renaissance sketch of flight to the suit and shelter that keep
astronauts safe from radiation on Mars. MARSOPS continues that line.

Allowed patterns:
- the old style's language: real archival images and film (fact-checked, commercial use allowed) with a slow push,
  cross-dissolves, one classy caption style (Playfair Display Italic, warm ivory, one line, the same spot, a gentle
  fade and rise), a soft shutter on every caption (the client's own shutter recording), music that carries the film;
- the blueprint: white construction lines that draw themselves over the real images; the renaissance paper cooling
  into blueprint blue (cyanotype toning of the modern images: the blueprint's own historical process);
- neon blue only for protection (the shield drawn around the suit, the habitat's shell, the line under MARSOPS);
- the MARSOPS wordmark rebuilt as live type (Montserrat 800, 0.10 em, measured on the supplied raster).
